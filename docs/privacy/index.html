Privacy Policy

How information provided to this campaign website is handled.

Effective date: September 17, 2026.

Introduction

Shawn Packard for State Representative District 64 (“the Campaign,” “we,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This policy describes how we collect and use information provided through this website and related campaign communications. It should be read with our Form Information notice and Text Message Terms.

Information We Collect

The contact form collects your name, email address, message, and any optional phone number you enter. If you choose the separate text-message checkbox, the form also records that opt-in request. The email-updates form collects your name, email address, and request for campaign email updates. If you interact with the Campaign through events or volunteer activities outside this site, the Campaign may request other information needed for those activities.

Our website hosting and form-delivery providers may also process technical information, such as browser or device details, pages requested, referral information, and information needed for security and spam prevention.

How We Use and Share Information

We may use information to respond to you, provide requested campaign updates, record your text-message choice, coordinate campaign activities, maintain records, and meet applicable legal obligations. We may use service providers working on our behalf for website hosting, form delivery, and message delivery.

Text Messaging

If you provide a mobile number and separately opt in, the Campaign may send recurring texts about updates, events, volunteering, fundraising, and voting. Message frequency may vary; message and data rates may apply. If you receive campaign texts, reply STOP to opt out or HELP for assistance. We will not sell or share mobile information or text-message opt-in consent with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. Providers needed to deliver our messages may process that information on our behalf. This website does not itself send texts.

Form Delivery and Retention

Website forms are handled by FormSubmit for forwarding to ShawnPackardfor64@gmail.com. FormSubmit says it retains submissions for up to 30 days. Delivery to the Campaign inbox has not been confirmed; please email the Campaign directly if you need to ensure receipt. Copies received in the Campaign inbox may remain there. Please do not send payment-card details, government identifiers, or other sensitive information through these forms.

Contributions

The Donate page uses a Stripe Buy Button and Stripe-hosted checkout. Stripe collects the contribution amount, email address, full name, billing address, occupation, employer or principal place of business, and payment details. Stripe processes the payment and makes donor and transaction information available to the Campaign for campaign-finance records. The Campaign website does not receive your payment-card number. Stripe's handling of information is also described in its Privacy Policy. Do not send payment-card details by email or through a website contact form.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or a request to withdraw an email-update or text-message request may be sent to ShawnPackardfor64@gmail.com or 207-431-8012.

Shawn Packard is fighting to protect Maine’s youth, strengthen our communities, and bring common-sense leadership back to Augusta.
