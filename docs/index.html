Meet Shawn

About Shawn Packard

From foster care to founding a business, Shawn’s path has been shaped by perseverance, faith, and service. His childhood in Maine’s foster system informs his commitment to improving it. He is a husband, father, entrepreneur, and advocate for families who feel unheard in Augusta.

His community work includes telling Mainers’ stories, supporting small businesses, and volunteering locally. The campaign emphasizes practical leadership, protecting children, and integrity in public service.

Shawn’s priorities include foster-care reform, Maine’s natural resources, practical education, and compassionate responses to addiction and homelessness.

“I’m here to protect our kids, our communities, and our Maine values.” — Shawn P.
