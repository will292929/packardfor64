Form Information

How contact messages and email update requests are handled.

Last updated September 17, 2026.

What you provide

The contact form asks for your name, email address, message, and an optional phone number. It also offers a separate, optional checkbox to request campaign text messages. The updates form asks for your name and email address and records your request for campaign email updates; it does not enroll you in text messages.

Where it goes

When you submit either form, the information goes to FormSubmit, an external form-delivery service, for forwarding to ShawnPackardfor64@gmail.com. FormSubmit says it retains submissions for up to 30 days. Copies received in the campaign mailbox may remain there.

Form delivery to the campaign inbox has not been confirmed. To make sure your message is received, email the address above directly or call 207-431-8012.

Your choices

Do not include payment-card details, government identifiers, or other sensitive information in these forms. To ask about a submission or withdraw an email-update or text-message request, contact the campaign at the email address above. Read the Privacy Policy and Text Message Terms for more information.
